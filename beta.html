01 — The Origin

It started with vibes.

Gamers across Canada and the US. No website, no bots, no automation — just game chat, shits and giggles.

02 — The Build

Then we built the machine.

PSN bots that never miss a clip. WhatsApp relays that deliver them in minutes. A pipeline that cuts reels and posts them to Instagram while we sleep. A local AI lab on our own hardware that transcribes our comms and coaches our game. The clan runs on software we wrote ourselves… and AI, of course.

03 — The Coach

The coach never sleeps.

Send any clip back with “rev” and the machine takes over — it watches every frame, transcribes your squad's voice comms, and hands you a graded report with what you did right, what you did wrong, and how to fix it. Private to you. Ruthless when it has to be.
